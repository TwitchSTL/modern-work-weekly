## The Week at a Glance

🟡 **Purview Data Lifecycle Management billing is shifting to volume-based pricing** - organizations retaining AI-generated content (including third-party AI prompts and responses) will now be charged by data volume, not by policy count, which could increase compliance costs depending on retention scope.

🟢 **People Skills data deletion control is now available** - administrators can permanently remove all employee skills data from the tenant, supporting data governance and offboarding needs.

🟢 **SharePoint auto-labeling for data at rest is coming in preview** - default sensitivity labels will soon apply automatically to existing files in SharePoint libraries, closing a common labeling gap without user action.

🟢 **Cloud PC and virtual desktop connectivity is more resilient** - RDP Multipath with redundant TCP is now generally available, reducing session interruptions for remote workers on Windows 365 and Azure Virtual Desktop.

*Sources: [Microsoft 365 Roadmap](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap) · [Windows 365 Tech Community Blog](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/t5/windows-it-pro-blog/rdp-multipath-with-redundant-tcp-is-now-generally-available-for/ba-p/4534610)*

## Why This Week Matters

The most consequential change this week is a billing model shift inside Microsoft Purview, one that could quietly increase compliance costs for any organization retaining AI-generated content under a data lifecycle policy. Finance and compliance teams should review current retention scope now, before costs compound. Alongside that, a new admin control for People Skills deletion gives organizations meaningful leverage over employee data, which is directly relevant to GDPR, state privacy laws, and any organization managing workforce data governance commitments.

*Sources: [Microsoft 365 Roadmap - DLM Meter Change](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?id=560324) · [Microsoft 365 Roadmap - People Skills Deletion Control](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?id=565905)*

## Risk & Compliance

🟡 **[Purview Data Lifecycle Management: Volume-Based Billing](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?id=560324):** Retention billing for non-Microsoft 365 AI content (including third-party generative AI prompts and responses) has shifted to a per-gigabyte model at $0.25/GB/month; organizations with broad AI retention policies should audit their data volumes now to avoid budget surprises, and ensure retention scope is defensible for SOC 2 and GDPR purposes.

🟢 **[People Skills Data Deletion Admin Control](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?id=565905):** A new admin-only control allows permanent deletion of all employee skills data from the tenant, including confirmed, inferred, and imported skills; this capability supports GDPR data subject rights and broader data minimization obligations, and compliance officers should incorporate it into offboarding and data governance procedures.

🟢 **[SharePoint Auto-Labeling for Existing Files at Rest](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?id=559105):** Preview launches in September 2026, with general availability in October 2026; this will automatically apply default sensitivity labels to unlabeled SharePoint files, which closes a common gap in information protection posture and reduces manual labeling risk for organizations subject to HIPAA, CMMC, or internal data classification requirements.

🟢 **[Microsoft and AXA XL Cyber Insurance Incident Response Partnership](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/blog/2026/07/22/real-world-incident-response-microsoft-and-axa-xl-strengthen-cyber-resilience/):** Microsoft Incident Response services are now available directly to AXA XL cyber insurance policyholders; organizations holding AXA XL policies should confirm with their broker whether this service is included in their coverage, as faster coordinated response can reduce breach costs and support cyber insurance claim outcomes.

*Sources: [Microsoft 365 Roadmap - DLM Meter Change](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?id=560324) · [Microsoft Security Blog - AXA XL Partnership](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/blog/2026/07/22/real-world-incident-response-microsoft-and-axa-xl-strengthen-cyber-resilience/) · [Microsoft 365 Roadmap - SharePoint Auto-Labeling](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?id=559105)*

## What Your Employees Will Notice

- **Remote desktop workers on Windows 365 or Azure Virtual Desktop** should experience fewer session drops and disconnections, particularly employees on VPNs, restricted networks, or variable home internet connections. No action is required on their part.
- **Employees who use Microsoft 365 skills features** (profile cards, Viva, Copilot skills suggestions) will see no change unless an administrator initiates a skills data deletion. If your organization chooses to offboard from People Skills, employees will notice that skills no longer appear on their profiles or in Copilot experiences.
- **SharePoint users will not need to manually label existing files** once auto-labeling rolls out later this year. Labels will be applied automatically based on the library defaults administrators have configured.

## Cost & Licensing

- **Purview Data Lifecycle Management pricing has changed.** The billing model for retaining non-Microsoft 365 generative AI content (prompts and responses from third-party AI tools) now charges $0.25 per gigabyte per month based on the total volume of retained text. Organizations that have applied DLM retention policies broadly to AI-generated content could see meaningful cost increases depending on volume. Finance and compliance teams should request a retention scope review from IT before the next billing cycle.
- **AXA XL policyholders may have new incident response coverage.** If your cyber insurance is through AXA XL, confirm with your broker whether Microsoft Incident Response services are now included. This could affect both your incident response planning and the value calculation of your current policy.

*Sources: [Microsoft 365 Roadmap - DLM Meter Change](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?id=560324) · [Microsoft Security Blog - AXA XL Partnership](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/blog/2026/07/22/real-world-incident-response-microsoft-and-axa-xl-strengthen-cyber-resilience/)*

## If You Take No Action

**On the Purview DLM billing change:** Organizations that do not audit their AI content retention scope risk absorbing unanticipated monthly charges as AI usage and data volumes grow. What may have been a flat, policy-count-based cost could now scale with every retained prompt and response across third-party AI tools. Without a deliberate review, this cost will grow silently.

**On SharePoint auto-labeling:** If default sensitivity labels on SharePoint libraries have not been configured correctly, the September preview could automatically apply incorrect or overly permissive labels to large volumes of existing files. For organizations subject to HIPAA, CMMC, or internal data classification requirements, this creates audit exposure that is harder to remediate after the fact than before.

*Sources: [Microsoft 365 Roadmap - DLM Meter Change](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?id=560324) · [Microsoft 365 Roadmap - SharePoint Auto-Labeling](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?id=559105)*
