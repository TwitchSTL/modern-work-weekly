## Top 5 This Week

1. **Purview DLM billing model changes to volume-based pricing** - If you retain non-Microsoft 365 generative AI prompts and responses under a DLM policy, your costs are now calculated per GB of retained data ($0.25/GB/month), not per message count. Review your retention policy scope and data volumes before your next billing cycle.

2. **RDP Multipath with redundant TCP is GA for Windows 365 and AVD** - This directly improves session resilience for users behind restrictive firewalls, VPNs, or UDP-blocked networks. No more silent session drops due to single-path TCP failures; enable it now for your Cloud PC fleet.

3. **People Skills admin deletion control is GA** - Admins can now permanently wipe the entire organizational skills library, including confirmed, inferred, and imported skills. This is the offboarding lever you needed for data governance and compliance audits against the People Skills feature.

4. **Auto-labeling of existing SharePoint files at rest is coming** - The roadmap item for applying default library sensitivity labels to data at rest lands in preview in September and GA in October 2026. Start auditing your library default label configurations now so rollout is clean.

5. **Microsoft IR services extended to AXA XL policyholders** - If your organization holds an AXA XL cyber insurance policy, Microsoft Incident Response is now a covered service. Worth surfacing to your CISO and legal teams before you need it.

## Security & Compliance

- **[New AI features in Chrome](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/NjkVAM_dE2w)** [GA] - Google Chrome on-device AI demo showcasing local data processing for personalized experiences. This is Google developer content, not a Microsoft security change; however, if your organization manages Chrome via policy, understand that on-device AI model capabilities are expanding and may warrant a review of your browser data handling policies.

- **[How to get Chrome Web Store approval](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/4FvIlVWWCnE)** [GA] - Chrome extension submission guidance from Google. Relevant only if your team maintains internal Chrome extensions; no direct M365 security implication, but Chrome extension governance intersects with your browser security baseline and any extension allowlisting policies in Defender for Endpoint.

- **[Real world incident response: Microsoft and AXA XL strengthen cyber resilience](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/blog/2026/07/22/real-world-incident-response-microsoft-and-axa-xl-strengthen-cyber-resilience/)** [GA] - Microsoft Incident Response services are now available directly to AXA XL cyber insurance policyholders, integrating technical response with insurance coordination. If your org holds AXA XL coverage, confirm with your risk and legal teams that Microsoft IR is enabled under your policy terms before an incident forces the question.

- **[RDP Multipath with redundant TCP is now generally available for Windows 365 and Azure Virtual Desktop](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/t5/windows-it-pro-blog/rdp-multipath-with-redundant-tcp-is-now-generally-available-for/ba-p/4534610)** [GA] - Session resilience for Cloud PCs and AVD sessions now extends to TCP-based paths, addressing packet loss, proxy interference, and UDP-blocked network segments. This eliminates one of the most common causes of session interruption in enterprise remote desktop deployments; validate your Windows 365 provisioning policies and AVD host pool configurations to confirm the feature is active.

- **[Microsoft Purview: Data Lifecycle Management - DLM Meter Change](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?id=560324)** [GA] - Billing for DLM policies covering non-Microsoft 365 generative AI prompts and responses shifts from a per-message model to per-GB retained ($0.25/GB/month). Audit the volume of AI-generated content currently under DLM policies and project your cost delta before the change hits your tenant's billing meter.

- **[Microsoft Copilot (Microsoft 365): People Skills - removal and deletion admin control](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?id=565905)** [GA] - A new admin-only control in People Skills settings allows permanent deletion of the entire organizational skills library, including all confirmed, inferred, and imported skills data. Once triggered, skills disappear from profile cards and all skills-based M365 experiences including Copilot and Viva; this is irreversible, so treat it as a formal offboarding action with appropriate change control.

- **[Microsoft Purview: Information Protection - Apply default SharePoint library labels to data at rest](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?id=559105)** [Preview] - Auto-labeling of existing SharePoint files will apply the default sensitivity label set on their parent document library, closing the gap for unlabeled data at rest without requiring user action. Preview lands September 2026, GA in October 2026; now is the time to audit and correct default label assignments on SharePoint libraries so auto-labeling applies the right classifications when the feature rolls out.

## Action Required

- **[Purview DLM Meter Change](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?id=560324)** [GA, billing impact now] - Pull a report of all DLM policies scoping non-Microsoft 365 generative AI content and calculate retained data volume in GB. The new $0.25/GB/month rate is live; unreviewed policies with high AI content volume could produce unexpected cost increases in your next billing cycle.

- **[RDP Multipath with redundant TCP - GA for Windows 365 and AVD](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/t5/windows-it-pro-blog/rdp-multipath-with-redundant-tcp-is-now-generally-available-for/ba-p/4534610)** [GA, enable now] - Verify the feature is enabled across your Windows 365 and AVD deployments, particularly for user populations on VPN or in UDP-restricted network environments. This is a reliability improvement that requires no licensing change but may need explicit configuration depending on your host pool and client version.

- **[SharePoint default library label auto-labeling for data at rest](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?id=559105)** [Preview September 2026, GA October 2026] - Within the next 60 days, audit default sensitivity label assignments across all SharePoint document libraries. Incorrect or missing default labels will be auto-applied at scale when this feature activates, and remediating mislabeled data post-rollout is significantly more effort than correcting the library defaults now.
