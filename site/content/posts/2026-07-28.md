## Top 5 This Week

1. **DLM Billing Model Change (GA)** - Purview Data Lifecycle Management is switching to volume-based billing ($0.25/GB/month). If your org retains non-Microsoft 365 generative AI prompts, your DLM costs are now tied directly to data volume. Run a sizing estimate before your next billing cycle.

2. **RDP Multipath Redundant TCP Now GA** - Windows 365 and Azure Virtual Desktop environments can now benefit from TCP-based RDP Multipath, closing the gap for UDP-restricted or proxy-heavy networks. This is a meaningful reliability win for enterprises with strict egress controls.

3. **People Skills Admin Deletion Control (GA)** - Admins can now permanently purge all People Skills data from the tenant, including inferred, confirmed, and imported skills. Critical for organizations with strict data governance or that are offboarding from People Skills experiences.

4. **Default SharePoint Library Label Auto-Apply Coming to Preview** - Existing files in SharePoint will be able to inherit their document library's default sensitivity label automatically. Preview lands in September 2026, GA in October 2026. Start auditing your library label configurations now.

5. **Microsoft + AXA XL IR Partnership** - Microsoft Incident Response services are now available directly to AXA XL cyber insurance policyholders. Relevant for organizations whose IR retainer or cyber insurance is routed through AXA XL.

## Security & Compliance

- **[Real World Incident Response: Microsoft and AXA XL Strengthen Cyber Resilience](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/blog/2026/07/22/real-world-incident-response-microsoft-and-axa-xl-strengthen-cyber-resilience/)** [GA] - If your organization carries AXA XL cyber insurance, Microsoft Incident Response services are now bundled in, enabling coordinated technical, business, and insurance response from a single engagement. Review your cyber insurance policy and IR retainer agreements to understand whether this partnership applies to your coverage. Worth surfacing to your CISO and legal teams as it may affect how IR engagements are scoped and billed.

- **[RDP Multipath with Redundant TCP is Now Generally Available for Windows 365 and Azure Virtual Desktop](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/t5/windows-it-pro-blog/rdp-multipath-with-redundant-tcp-is-now-generally-available-for/ba-p/4534610)** [GA] - Enterprises running Windows 365 or AVD in environments with packet loss, VPN routing, UDP restrictions, or proxy configurations now have a production-ready resiliency path via TCP-based RDP Multipath. This GA release extends a capability previously limited to UDP-capable paths, directly addressing session interruption complaints in restrictive network topologies. Validate that your Cloud PC deployments are on a client and host build that supports this feature, and test in environments where UDP has historically caused stability issues.

- **[Microsoft Purview: Data Lifecycle Management - DLM Meter Change](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?id=560324)** [GA] - DLM billing is moving from a per-message to a volume-based model, charging $0.25 per GB of retained data per month. Any non-Microsoft 365 generative AI prompts and responses retained under a DLM policy are now metered by total text volume rather than message count. Run a data volume estimate against your current DLM policies before the billing change hits, particularly if you have broad AI prompt retention policies configured.

- **[Microsoft Copilot (Microsoft 365): People Skills - Removal and Deletion Admin Control](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?id=565905)** [GA] - A new admin control in People Skills settings allows permanent, tenant-wide deletion of all skills data, including inferred, confirmed, and imported skills. Once triggered, skills disappear from profile cards and are excluded from Copilot and Viva skills-based experiences, with no automatic recovery path. Organizations considering an offboard from People Skills or facing data residency and compliance requirements around skills inference should document this control in their data governance runbooks now.

- **[Microsoft Purview: Information Protection - Apply Default SharePoint Library Labels to Data at Rest](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?id=559105)** [Preview] - Starting September 2026, Purview will auto-apply a document library's default sensitivity label to existing unlabeled files in SharePoint, with GA following in October 2026. This closes a long-standing gap where data at rest escaped labeling coverage unless users manually applied labels. Begin auditing your SharePoint library default label configurations now so auto-labeling doesn't produce unexpected label assignments on sensitive libraries that have incorrect or missing defaults set.

## Action Required

- **[DLM Meter Change - Volume-Based Billing](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?id=560324)** [GA - review before next billing cycle] - The shift to $0.25/GB/month for retained DLM data means any broad AI prompt retention policy could produce materially higher costs than the prior per-message model. Pull retention policy scope and estimate data volumes retained across non-Microsoft 365 generative AI connectors before your next invoice lands. Adjust policy retention periods or scope if volume projections are unexpectedly large.

- **[People Skills Deletion Control - Governance Documentation](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?id=565905)** [GA - action within 30 days] - Now that a permanent, admin-initiated deletion path exists for all People Skills data, update your data governance and offboarding runbooks to include this control. Establish approval workflows and access controls around who in your tenant has permission to trigger a full skills data purge, since the action is irreversible and has no automatic recovery.

- **[SharePoint Library Default Label Auto-Apply - Audit Library Configurations](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?id=559105)** [Preview September 2026 - prep now] - Before the September preview enables auto-labeling of existing SharePoint files, audit all document libraries with default sensitivity labels configured. Libraries with incorrect or missing defaults will apply those labels at scale to existing files when the feature activates. Remediate label misconfigurations now to avoid unexpected label assignments across sensitive or regulated content stores.
